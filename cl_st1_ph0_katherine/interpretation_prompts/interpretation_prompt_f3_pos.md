>This range of macro-level discursive constructs is referred to as *ideological discourses*. For our purposes, by ideological discourses, we mean socially shared, socially situated representations of real-world phenomena conveyed implicitly through language use. Because they are shared, ideological discourses also constrain or limit how real-world phenomena are represented.
